ATTRIBUTION
===========

Strata's built-in textures bundle selections from Pixel Perfection
Fidelity: a cozy medieval pack faithfully based on XSSheep's Pixel
Perfection, maintained past 1.11 with the original textures and
desaturated artstyle kept intact. No textures here are original to this
project.

- Pixel Perfection, by XSSheep — Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike
  4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Pixel Perfection Fidelity (the updated pack these files come from),
  including its PPL Hodgepodge selections.

Filenames were adjusted to this engine's manifest; pixel content is used
as distributed.

Per the Share-Alike terms, this bundle is likewise shared under
CC BY-SA 4.0: keep this file with the textures, credit as above, and
share derivatives under the same license.
